About the Website

TripAd-Vice-or is a humorous web application that presents a collection of bad habits in a playful and entertaining way. Users can browse through different bad habits, search for specific ones, filter them by category and interact with them by pressing the “Bad Idea” button. The application is designed to combine humor with simple awareness, showing how everyday choices can sometimes lead to funny, risky or questionable outcomes. Through its clean interface and interactive features, TripAd-Vice-or offers a lighthearted browsing experience while encouraging users to think twice before following a “bad idea”.
